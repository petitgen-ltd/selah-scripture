SELAH · NOT JUST ATHLETES

Wherever a heart races — or finally rests.

The same wearable, the same heartbeat sensor. Six lives, six moments — each one met by the word already waiting for it.
The wall, on a run
Heart-rate spikes, effort climbs toward the limit.
“I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me.” Philippians 4:13
Cardiac rehab
A heart relearning how to beat, watched closely.
“Be still, and know that I am God.” Psalm 46:10
The ninth hour of labor
Racing pulse, rising waves, and no free hands.
“Do not fear, for I am with you.” Isaiah 41:10
The 3 a.m. grief walk
Elevated resting heart-rate, another sleepless night.
“…through the valley… you are with me.” Psalm 23:4
A nurse’s twelfth hour
Sustained strain, running on empty, still giving.
“Be strong and courageous.” Joshua 1:9
The finish · the quiet after
Recovery — the heart settling back down to rest.
“You ran your race today. Well done.” Selah · a moment of rest
One heartbeat, a thousand contexts — the right word, already there.
Selah  presence, not performance.
